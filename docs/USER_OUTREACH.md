## Guidelines for Community Outreach

- **Authenticity First**: Never claim adoption numbers or tester counts that have not yet been verified.
- **Clear Expectations**: Clearly inform testers that Midnight Vault operates on the **Midnight Preprod Testnet** and requires testnet $tNIGHT / DUST.
